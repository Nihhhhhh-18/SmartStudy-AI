## Error Analysis

### What improved compared with V1
- The response explicitly notes when the supplied notes do not define a term in detail.
- It avoids adding detailed procedures for pruning or dropout.
- The output includes exactly five questions with answers.
- It includes a final grounding check.

### What still needs improvement
- Table formatting is broken: headings and cells appear as separate one-cell tables.
- Some key terms, such as “training data” and “validation,” are mentioned in the notes but not formally defined there. The response should distinguish a direct definition from a cautious paraphrase.
- The final grounding check is a claim made by the model; it should not be treated as independent proof that every statement is grounded.

### Changes to test in Prompt V3
1. Require simple Markdown bullet lists instead of tables, to reduce formatting failures.
2. Require the model to include only terms explicitly present in the notes and to quote or point to the supporting phrase for each definition when practical.
3. Keep exactly five questions with answers and retain a mixture of question types.
4. Keep the final output concise and easy to read.
