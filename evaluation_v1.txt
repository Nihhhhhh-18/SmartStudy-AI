## Error Analysis

### What worked well
- The output is clearly structured and easy to revise from.
- All six main lecture topics appear in the summary.
- Exactly five questions are provided, each with an answer.
- The questions use different formats.

### What could be improved
- Restrict the key-term section to terms explicitly present in the notes.
- Ensure every definition and answer is traceable to the source notes.
- Avoid adding explanations or concepts that the source does not state.

### Unsupported or questionable content to review
- “Machine Learning Model,” “Training Data,” “Unseen Data,” “Validation Data,” and “Model Complexity” are included as key terms, although the notes do not define all of these as separate terms.
- The phrase “data used to fit a model” appears in the generated output but is not given as a standalone definition in the notes.
- Some wording in the answers paraphrases the notes; paraphrases should preserve the original meaning without adding new claims.

### Changes to test in Prompt V2
1. Require the key-term list to include only terms explicitly present in the lecture notes.
2. Require each definition and answer to be supported directly by the notes; if the notes do not provide enough information, say so instead of guessing.
3. Keep exactly five practice questions and use a mixture of question types.
4. Add a simple source-grounding check before producing the final study pack.
