# Study Pack: Machine Learning — Overfitting and Regularization

## 1. Summary

### 1.1 Overfitting

Overfitting occurs when a machine learning model learns the training data too closely, including noise and unimportant patterns.

- It may perform very well on training data.
- It may perform poorly on unseen data.
- It memorizes training data instead of learning only meaningful patterns.

### 1.2 Underfitting

Underfitting occurs when a model fails to learn the important patterns in the training data.

- It typically performs poorly on training data.
- It also performs poorly on unseen data.

### 1.3 Regularization

Regularization is a technique used to reduce overfitting by discouraging excessive model complexity. It can help a model generalize better to unseen data.

### 1.4 Cross-Validation

Cross-validation evaluates a model by splitting available data into training and validation portions across multiple rounds.

It helps estimate how well the model may perform on data that was not used to fit it.

### 1.5 Other Methods to Reduce Overfitting

The notes identify three additional methods:

- Collecting more representative training data
- Pruning decision trees
- Using dropout in suitable neural networks

The supplied notes do not explain the detailed procedures for these methods.

### 1.6 Generalization

Generalization is a model's ability to perform well on new, unseen examples.

A useful machine learning model should learn meaningful patterns rather than simply memorize its training data.

## 3. Practice Questions

### Question 1 — Multiple Choice

Which statement best describes overfitting?

A. The model fails to learn important patterns in the training data.

B. The model learns the training data too closely, including noise and unimportant patterns.

C. The model is evaluated across multiple rounds.

D. The model performs poorly on both training and unseen data.

Answer: B. Overfitting occurs when a model learns the training data too closely, including noise and unimportant patterns.

### Question 2 — Short Answer

What is the difference between overfitting and underfitting?

Answer:

- Overfitting: The model learns the training data too closely and may perform very well on training data but poorly on unseen data.
- Underfitting: The model fails to learn important patterns and typically performs poorly on both training data and unseen data.
